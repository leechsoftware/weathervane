# Privacy and data sources

The full privacy policy is at
[leechsoftware.com/weathervane-for-ecowitt/privacy.html](https://leechsoftware.com/weathervane-for-ecowitt/privacy.html).
This page is a plain summary.

## What the app collects

Nothing. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account with us.

Your readings travel between your device and either your own gateway or Ecowitt Cloud.
Your history, records and settings are kept on your device.

## Your Ecowitt keys

The application key and API key are stored in your device's Keychain. They are sent only to
Ecowitt, to read your station. Your Ecowitt password is not kept.

## What is sent to weather services

To show a forecast, radar and warnings for the right place, the app sends your station's
location to the services listed below. It sends nothing else about you or your station.

## Contacting us

**Contact us** in the About screen sends what you type, with a short report about the app
and your devices: the app and system version, the kind of gateway and sensors, and the names
of the fields your device reports. It never includes readings, keys, passwords or MAC
addresses.

## Where the weather data comes from

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| Your station's readings | Your Ecowitt gateway or console, or [Ecowitt Cloud](https://www.ecowitt.net) |
| 5-day forecast, rain by the hour, average rainfall | [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) |
| Written forecasts and warnings, United States | [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov) |
| Radar and tides, United States | [NOAA](https://www.noaa.gov) |
| Warnings, Germany | [Deutscher Wetterdienst](https://www.dwd.de) |
| Warnings, rest of Europe | [MeteoAlarm](https://www.meteoalarm.org) and the national services behind it |
| Radar outside the United States | [RainViewer](https://www.rainviewer.com) |

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com is offered under the
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence.

Weathervane is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ecowitt or
any of these services.
