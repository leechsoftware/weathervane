# Supported hardware

Weathervane works with Ecowitt gateways and consoles, the stations built on them, and the
sensors paired to them. What you see depends on your gateway, its firmware and your sensors.

## Gateways

GW1100, GW1200, GW2000, GW3000, WS6210 and WS6006.

The original GW1000 works through Ecowitt Cloud only.

## Consoles

HP25xx, HP3500, WS2320, WS2910, WS38xx, WS39xx, WN18xx and WN19xx.

Some consoles, such as the HP2560 and HP2564, can only be read through Ecowitt Cloud. The app
says so the first time you add one.

## Stations

Any station built on these gateways and consoles, including Wittboy and Essense3. Newer
models usually work too.

## Sensors

Every sensor paired to your gateway gets a card of its own, which you can rename and reorder.

| Sensor | What it measures |
|---|---|
| WS80, WS85, WS90 (Wittboy) | Outdoor sensor arrays |
| WH65, WS69 | Outdoor sensor arrays |
| WH31, WN31 | Temperature and humidity |
| WN34 | Temperature probe |
| WH51 | Soil moisture |
| WN35 | Leaf wetness |
| WH40, WN40 | Rain gauge |
| WH41, WH43 | PM2.5 |
| WH45 | Air quality and CO2 |
| WH55 | Water leak |
| WH57 | Lightning |
| WN36 | Black globe temperature |
| LDS01 | Water level and distance |

The indoor sensors inside the gateway or console are shown too.

## Two rain gauges

A station with both a Wittboy array and a separate rain gauge shows both, and Settings lets
you choose which one is the main gauge.

## Something missing?

If a sensor or reading does not appear, use **Contact us** in the app's About screen. It
attaches a list of what your device reports (field names only, never your readings or keys),
which is usually enough to add support. You can also
[open an issue](https://github.com/leechsoftware/weathervane/issues/new/choose).
