# Getting started

## Install the app

Weathervane is free on the App Store, where it is listed as **Dashboard for ECOWITT
Weather**: [apps.apple.com/app/id6812456607](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6812456607).

One listing covers iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

| Device | Needs |
|---|---|
| iPhone | iOS 17 or later |
| iPad | iPadOS 17 or later |
| Mac | macOS 14 or later |
| Apple Watch | watchOS 10 or later |

The app is not distributed anywhere else. There is no download in this repository.

## The first screen

When you open the app for the first time it asks how you would like to connect:

- **Ecowitt Cloud.** Works from anywhere, for any station on your Ecowitt account.
- **Find devices on this Wi-Fi network.** Reads a gateway or console directly. It only
  reaches devices on the same network, so it cannot get readings when you are away from home.
- **Just show me a sample dashboard.** Demo mode, described below.

[Connecting your station](connecting.md) explains the two ways to connect and which to pick.

## Demo mode

Demo mode fills the whole app with sample readings: a station called Back Garden, a second
station, a gateway and a set of sensors. Nothing is sent or received, and no account is
needed. It is the quickest way to see whether the app suits you before setting anything up.

A green bar across the top says **Demo mode — sample data** for as long as it is on. Tap
**Exit** on that bar to go back to the first screen.

## After connecting

- **More than one station?** Tap the station's name at the top of the dashboard to switch.
- **Rearranging.** Cards, tiles and charts can be reordered, shown or hidden in Settings.
- **Units.** The app starts with the units your region uses. Change any of them in Settings.
- **Alerts.** They are set up in the Alerts screen. See [Alerts](alerts.md).
