# Features

## The dashboard

The top card is the outdoor temperature on a thermometer, with today's high and low marked
on it, the sky, humidity, and a line for the last 24 hours. Under the temperature it says how
today compares with yesterday and whether the temperature is rising or falling.

Below that, a tile for each reading your station has:

- **Indoor** temperature and humidity
- **Wind** with the day's maximum and the wind in plain words on the Beaufort scale
- **Rain Today** against an average month
- **Pressure** with its trend and a barometer outlook
- **Solar** with the hours of sunshine and the share of a clear sky's light
- **UV Index** with the day's peak

Tiles show a bar so you can read them at a glance. A tile only appears when the station
reports that reading.

## Cards

- **Wind.** A compass with the speed in its centre and a pointer at the direction the wind is
  coming from now. Behind it, a heat map shows where the last day's wind came from, coloured
  by how hard it blew. Gust, the day's maximum and wind run sit beside it.
- **Rainfall.** Event, hour, today, week, month and year, with when it last rained and how
  many dry days have followed.
- **Comfort.** Feels like, dew point and how the air feels, with Humidex, cloud base and, in
  hot weather, the wet-bulb temperature and heat risk.
- **Garden.** Water lost against rain, soil moisture, growing degree days, chill hours and
  vapour-pressure deficit, and a note when the garden needs water.
- **Sun and Moon.** Sunrise, solar noon, sunset, day length against yesterday, dawn and dusk,
  the moon's phase with moonrise and moonset, and tides for US coasts.
- **Stations.** Your other stations with their temperature. Tap one to switch.
- **Sensors.** A card for every add-on sensor with its readings, battery, signal and a graph.

Cards, tiles and charts can be reordered, shown or hidden.

## Forecast

A 5-day forecast for your station's location with the chance of rain for each day.

- **Adjusted to your station.** Once the app has about ten days of your readings, the highs
  and lows are adjusted by how your own station has read against the forecast over the last
  month, and Today follows what your station has already measured. A line under the forecast
  says what was added. It can be turned off in Settings.
- **Rain by the hour.** Tap a day to see each hour's chance of rain and amount.
- **Written forecasts** from the National Weather Service, in the United States.

## Radar and warnings

Live radar, and severe weather warnings from the National Weather Service in the United
States and from national weather services in Europe. See [Coverage by region](regions.md).

## History

Charts for every reading, from one hour to a month, and up to three years with Ecowitt
Cloud. Tap a chart for the exact value and time, or open it full screen. The pressure chart
turns green while pressure is rising and red while it is falling.

## Records

Highs, lows and totals from today back to all time, the longest dry and wet spells, wind run,
and a month-by-month year. There is a monthly report, and readings can be exported as CSV.

## Stations and settings

- As many stations as you have, on Wi-Fi or Ecowitt Cloud.
- Fahrenheit or Celsius, and units for pressure, wind, rain, solar and distance.
- 12-hour or 24-hour time, and a weather day that starts at midnight or 9 am.
- Light or dark appearance.
- A two-column layout on iPad, with keyboard shortcuts.
- Firmware status for every device, with Ecowitt's release notes when an update is out.
- English and Swedish, following your device's language.
