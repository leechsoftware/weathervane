# Coverage by region

Everything your station measures works everywhere. The forecast, radar and warnings come
from public weather services, and those differ by country.

| | United States | Europe | Elsewhere |
|---|---|---|---|
| Station readings, tiles, cards, history, records | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| 5-day forecast with the chance of rain | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Rain by the hour | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Written forecast in words | Yes, in English | No | No |
| Severe weather warnings | Yes | Yes, in the countries listed below | No |
| Radar | Yes | Yes | Where radar coverage exists |
| Tides | Yes, near a coast | No | No |
| Sun, moon, daylight | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Rain against an average month | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Warnings in Europe

Germany, and the countries whose national services publish through MeteoAlarm: Austria,
Belgium, Bosnia-Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland,
France, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg,
Malta, Moldova, Montenegro, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania,
Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland and Ukraine.

Warnings are shown in the app's language where the service provides it, and otherwise in
English. The United Kingdom is not covered yet.

## Units and the weather day

The app starts with the units your region uses, and you can change any of them. The day's
totals can run from midnight or from 9 am, as used in Australia, New Zealand, the United
Kingdom and Ireland.

## Air quality

The US AQI scale by default, and the European Air Quality Index in Europe.

## Languages

English and Swedish. The app follows your device's language.
