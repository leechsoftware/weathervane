# Apple Watch, widgets and Siri

## Apple Watch

Any of your stations on your wrist: the outdoor temperature with humidity, today's high and
low, and indoor temperature, rain, wind and UV underneath.

**Complications** for your watch face include the temperature in a ring from today's low to
high, the last 24 hours as a line, and **One Reading**, which you can set to any reading:
wind on a dial, humidity or UV on a gauge, the next sunrise or sunset, or rain, pressure,
feels like, dew point or indoor.

With Ecowitt Cloud the watch reads your station itself, without your iPhone. A station
connected over Wi-Fi only reaches the watch through the iPhone.

## Widgets

- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, including a large one with wind, rain, pressure, UV,
  solar and indoor under the temperature.
- A widget for each station: edit a widget and pick its station.
- Widgets for individual sensors.

## Live Activities

A Live Activity appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while it rains or
lightning is near, and for a Severe or Extreme weather warning while the app is open.

## Siri, Shortcuts and Control Center

Ask for current conditions, the temperature, rain, how windy it is, the humidity, the
pressure or the indoor temperature "in Weathervane". The same actions are in the Shortcuts
app. On iOS 18 and later there is a Weathervane button for Control Center.

## Sharing
