# What's new

The lists below follow what the App Store shows for each version. The complete list for every
version is under **Version History** on the
[App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6812456607).

## 2.7

Released 10 November 2026.

- **Forecast is based on your station:** The 5-day highs and lows are adjusted by how your own station has read against the forecast over the last month, and Today follows what your station has already measured.
- **Wind compass:** A larger colorful compass that shows where the direction the wind came from, colored by how hard it blew, with the speed in its centre and a pointer at the direction it is coming from now.
- **Daily summary:** Now says how yesterday's forecast compared with what your station measured.
- **Reading bars:** Redesigned, the Indoor, Wind, Rain, Pressure and Solar tiles gain a bar.
- **Indoor temp/humidity card:** Added a new card, indoor temperature and humidity at the end of History.
- **Stations list card:** Added a new card, a small card that lists all your other stations with their temperature and details. Tap one to switch.
- **Barometer card trend graph change:** The pressure chart turns green while rising and red while falling.
- **Localization:** Added new language - Swedish
- **Devices over LAN:** Added gateway's memory, uptime, clock and calibration, with warnings about repeated restarts.
- **Comfort card:** Added Humidex, cloud base, and how far the air has travelled today.
- **Garden card:** Added chill hours and vapour-pressure deficit.
- **Sun and moon card:** Added solar noon, day length against yesterday, dawn and dusk.
- **Tides:** Added the next high and low tides at the nearest NOAA station (US coasts), in the moon card expanse.
- **Records:** Longest dry and wet spells, wind run and a month-by-month year.
- **Air quality:** The European Air Quality Index.
- **Camera:** added timelapse links.
- **Graphs:** Cosmetic changes to all the graphs in the cards
- **Steadier readings:** One bad packet no longer becomes a record, an alert or a day's rain.
- **New alerts:** Icy surfaces after rain, heat risk, UV index, wind chill, fog or dew, and hot tomorrow.
- **Sensor:** Snow depth is now smoothed.
- And many more little details...

**Fixes**

- One damaged value from a gateway no longer loses the whole reading.
- Opening the app no longer sends alerts based on the reading saved from last time.
- An update to a weather warning no longer sends a second alert, and a warning you've dismissed stays dismissed. One that gets worse still alerts.
- German warnings are graded as the DWD grades them, and withdrawn European warnings no longer show or alert.
- A station with one rain gauge no longer loses its rain history to an empty second one.
- A gateway given a new address by your router is recognized and keeps its history.
- History import recovers by itself if the network or gateway isn't ready when the app starts.
- Rain totals on the 6-month and 1-year charts add up to what fell.
- The "Garden needs water" alert now goes out for the station on screen.
- With a WS85 or WS90, the app and its rain alerts know it's raining from the first drops, including light drizzle that was being missed.
- A daily summary due during quiet hours arrives silently instead of being lost.
- A console that can only be read through Ecowitt Cloud, such as the HP2560 or HP2564, says so the first time you add it.
- Sunrise, sunset and day or night are right through the New Zealand summer, where the sun sets after midnight, and for a station in a far-off time zone.

## 2.6

Released 8 November 2026.

- **Apple Watch:** The complications are redrawn: the temperature in a ring from today's low to high, the day's range along the corner, the last 24 hours as a line, and the sky on every one.
- **One Reading, on the watch:** A complication you can set to any reading: wind on a dial, humidity or UV on a gauge, the next sunrise or sunset as a ring, or rain, pressure, feels like, dew point or indoor.
- **Large widget:** Wind, rain, pressure, UV, solar and indoor under the temperature.
- **A widget per station:** Edit any widget and pick its station.
- **New records:** An alert when a reading beats your station's highest or lowest temperature, strongest gust or wettest day.
- **Severe warnings on the Lock Screen:** A Severe or Extreme warning gets a Live Activity of its own while the app is open, with the time it runs until.
- **Barometer outlook:** The Pressure tile adds the Zambretti forecast, from "Settled fine" to "Stormy, much rain".
- **Days since rain:** The Rainfall card says when it last rained and how many dry days have followed; the tile shows the last 24 hours when the night's rain isn't in today's figure.
- **Sunshine:** The Solar tile counts today's hours of sunshine and the share of a clear sky's light arriving.
- **Garden:** Water lost against rain since the last soaking, and "Water the garden" when it is half an inch short, with an optional alert.
- **Heat stress:** In hot air the Comfort card gives the wet-bulb temperature and the heat risk in the shade.
- **Temperature trend:** "Rising, 4° in 3 h" under the temperature when the air is moving.
- **iPad:** Keyboard shortcuts for refresh, switching station and every screen, with Escape to close; tiles and buttons answer a trackpad.
- **Siri and Control Center:** Ask how windy it is, the humidity, the pressure or the indoor temperature "in Weathervane"; on iOS 18, a Weathervane button for Control Center.

**Fixes**

- "Device offline" is sent for a station that stops answering while the app is closed.
- On an iPad a full-screen chart no longer turns on its side, and the app opens in one window.
- On iOS 17 the temperature-probe and sign-in icons show again.
- The Lock Screen rain card says "Last reading 3:12 PM · tap to refresh" when it is old, and notes when Background App Refresh is off.
