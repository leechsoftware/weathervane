# Alerts

Alerts are notifications on your device. Each one has its own switch, and most have a limit
you can set.

## What can alert you

| Group | Alerts |
|---|---|
| Temperature | Freeze, frost tonight, icy surfaces after rain, high and low temperature, rapid drop, wind chill, heat risk, hot tomorrow |
| Rain | Rain starting and stopping, heavy rain, daily and event totals |
| Wind | Strong wind and strong gusts |
| Pressure | Falling or rising fast, a storm building |
| Lightning | Detected, and nearby |
| Air | Humidity, air quality, fog or dew likely, UV index |
| Garden | Garden needs water |
| Records | A new highest or lowest temperature, strongest gust or wettest day |
| Sensors | Limits you set on any sensor, low batteries, a sensor gone quiet |
| Station | Station offline, firmware updates |
| Warnings | Severe weather warnings from the National Weather Service and Europe's national services |

There is also a **daily summary** at a time you choose, which includes how yesterday's
forecast compared with what your station measured, and a **moon alert** at sunset.

## Water-leak alarm

If a leak sensor gets wet, you get an urgent alert with a loud alarm that repeats until you
have seen it. It works even with other alerts turned off.

## Quiet hours

During quiet hours, ordinary alerts are held back. Urgent ones, such as a water leak or
lightning nearby, still come through. A daily summary due during quiet hours arrives silently.

## When alerts are checked

- **While the app is open,** every station is checked as its readings arrive.
- **With the app closed on iPhone or iPad,** iOS decides when the app may check in the
  background. That can be every 15 minutes or only a few times a day, so an alert can arrive
  late. Keep Background App Refresh on for the app.
- **On a Mac,** the app stays in the menu bar and checks every station around the clock for
  as long as it is running. If timely alerts matter to you, the
  [Mac version](https://github.com/leechsoftware/weathervane-mac) is the dependable way to
  get them.

Alerts are worked out on your own device from your station's readings. Nothing is sent to a
server of ours.

## An alert did not arrive

1. Check that notifications are allowed for the app in the device's Settings.
2. Check that the alert's own switch is on, and that quiet hours do not cover the time.
3. On iPhone, check that Background App Refresh is on and Low Power Mode is off.
4. Most alerts are sent once and then not again for a few hours, so a second one may be
   held back on purpose.
